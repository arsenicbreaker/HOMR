# HOMR Landing Page Design Decisions

Design read: fintech RWA landing page for hackathon judges and prospective users, expressed through warm kawaii editorial graphics. Dials: ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 3, MOTION 2.

- Color: cream, ink, aqua, and coral turn the playful NeedMCP reference into a trustworthy but approachable Indonesia-focused finance identity.
- Layout: the centered hero follows the `kawaii-coffee-hero` blueprint, while later sections change composition to match the product narrative rather than repeat a card grid.
- Typography: Fraunces gives housing and capital a human editorial voice; Manrope keeps financial explanations readable; DM Mono marks system states and proof labels.
- Spacing: broad hero and section spacing keeps the dense risk narrative scannable, while ledger rows become tighter to communicate evidence and precision.
- Cards: only the two decisions use large cards because credit eligibility and capital allocation must be understood as separate systems.
- Motion: floating status notes preserve the source wireframe's signature energy and are disabled for reduced-motion users.
- Image: an Indonesian residential development gives immediate geographic and product context instead of using generic financial imagery.
